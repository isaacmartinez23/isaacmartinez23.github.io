# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS (chosen by the owner), served by GitHub Pages from `isaacmartinez23.github.io` with the custom domain `isaacmartinez.space`. The project dashboards live in other repos under `/analisis_profeco/`, `/ecobici/` and `/Pronostico-de-Demanda-y-Politica-de-Inventario/`. Do not break those paths.

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers for data analyst, data scientist and analytics roles, mostly in Mexico and in remote LATAM or US roles. They usually arrive from LinkedIn or a CV link, on a laptop at work or on a phone. In under a minute they decide whether Isaac is worth an interview.

## Product Purpose

Isaac Martínez Ríos's personal portfolio. A visitor should understand three things: he turns open data into business decisions backed by a measured result, his projects are reproducible end to end, and contacting him is easy (email, LinkedIn, CV).

## Positioning

He combines a business administration degree (UNAM) with end-to-end data work. Every project starts from a business question and ends in a decision, with its limitations stated: forecasting → inventory policy, open price data → household savings, bike-share trips → rebalancing.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual: Spanish is the default, and English is available through a language toggle on every page.
- Featured case studies: demand forecasting and inventory policy (M5 Walmart), PROFECO QQP food basket prices, and ECOBICI bike availability.
- Archive: Pizza sales dashboard, Chocolate sales dashboard, Users profile dashboard and Freshmart dashboard. All other old projects (Netflix, Penguins) are removed.
- The owner asked to remove the three headline hero figures. The first viewport does not lead with big metrics.
- Keep Google Analytics `G-0244QQM6F0`.

## Brand Commitments

- The owner picked the visual direction "Informe de análisis": an editorial analysis-report feel on a light ground.
- Name: Isaac Martínez Ríos.

## Evidence on Hand

- Project facts come only from the READMEs and memos in the three repos.
- Chart data is extracted to `data/figures.json` from each repo's published dashboard data.
- Dashboard screenshots and the archive thumbnails are in `assets/img/`. The graduation portrait is `assets/img/isaac.webp`.
- Platzi: 232 courses, 21 completed paths. Certificate links are on the profile `platzi.com/p/IsaacMartinez/`.
- CVs: `assets/images/Isaac Martinez CV.pdf` and `assets/images/Isaac Martinez CV english.pdf`. Both predate the new projects.
- There are no testimonials, employers or client names. Never invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the question and the decision, not the tool list.
2. Every number on the site traces back to a repo document.
3. State limitations; they are part of the credibility.
4. A recruiter must find contact and CV within seconds from any page.
